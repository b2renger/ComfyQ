# NOTES | notes | end

Depth 3 — three choices deep on every path. S0 offers two, S1_A offers **three**
and S1_B two, and all five depth-2 states offer two, so the graph is
1 + 2 + 5 + 5 endings = 13 generating states. Twelve paths across five endings.

Routing check: S0 → S1_A | S1_B. S1_A → S2_AA | S2_AB | S2_AC. S1_B → S2_BA |
S2_BB. Every depth-2 state routes to two endings, and every ending except E05
terminates; E05 routes back to S0, and it loops honestly — the clock it leaves
running is the one the film opens on, stopped.

Endings are shared on purpose, never to save shots. E02 is reached from S2_AA
(taking the key back up to read the clock) and from S2_AB (carrying the number
up) because both are the same act: bringing what the water said indoors. E03 is
reached from S2_AB and S2_AC because writing the number down and letting the
water find its way are both refusals to intervene. E04 from S2_AC and S2_BA:
following the pipe inland and cutting new teeth are both repairs that show.
E05 from S2_BA and S2_BB — setting it to the water and letting the sea keep the
time are the same surrender arrived at from the mechanism and from the water.

No choice is good against bad. Every card offers two ways of caring for the
same thing: read it or leave it, mend it or let it run, keep the time or give
the time away.

The protagonist is never seen face-on — from behind, or as hands. Three
locations, each opened wide before any close-up: the museum (S0), the basin
(S1_A), the mechanism room (S1_B). Ambience 1 runs under the museum states,
Ambience 2 under the basin, Ambience 3 under the mechanism room; the two
crossings are S1_A cut 1 and S1_B cut 1.

Clip length carries the pacing: 3 s for a single beat, 8 s for the four shots
that have to cover distance or change of state — the descent in S1_A, the train
taking up in S2_BA, the water closing over the knot in E01, and the walk out in
E05.
