# NOTES | notes | end

Linear: LIBRARY → IDLE → S0 → S1 → S2 → S3 → E01. No choices, so the only two
cards are the title and the close.

The protagonist is the lantern, not a person. Hands enter frame twice and are
never followed to a face, which removes face drift across nineteen cuts and
removes the content-filter surface from the cards entirely.

Three locations, each opened on a wide shot before any close-up of it: the lamp
room (S0), the cliff path (S1, and again under rain in S2), the tower gallery
(S3 and E01). Ambience 1 runs under S0, Ambience 2 under S1 and S2, Ambience 3
under S3 and E01 — the two crossings are S1 cut 1 and S3 cut 1.

Clip lengths carry the pacing rather than the cutting: 3 s for a single beat
(a wick catching, a hook lighting), 8 s for the three shots that have to cover
distance (the approach along the path, the walk to the tower, the final sweep).

The film argues that a light is kept alive by being handed on, so the last cut
is the beam and not the lantern, and the closing line names the flame rather
than the person carrying it.
